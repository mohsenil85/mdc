Dream Journal

Women remember more dreams.

1
The finding

175

studies, pooled into one analysis.

Women report higher dream recall than men. The gap opens in adolescence, peaks in our twenties, and narrows again with age.

Schredl & Reinhard, meta-analysis, 2008

2
Hold this loosely

The gap is an average.

Gender is a spectrum.

So take the whole finding with a grain of salt.

3
What does predict recall

Waking briefly in the night. You can only file a dream if you surface while it’s still there.

Activity at the temporoparietal junction — where the sense of self in space is built.

Simply being interested. The most boring finding, and the strongest one.

4
Speculation begins here

Why might it have gone this way?

The light sleeper. A body built to wake for an infant wakes more often — and each surfacing is another chance to carry a dream back.

The social rehearsal. If dreaming practices relationships, whoever held the group together had more to practice.

5
Still speculation

The threat drill. Dreams may rehearse danger — and men and women were not braced for the same dangers.

The detailed archive. Women recall waking memories with more scene and more feeling. Dreams may just ride along.
