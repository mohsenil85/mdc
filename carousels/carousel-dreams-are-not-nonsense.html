Dream Journal

“You know dreams are just nonsense, right?”

That’s what the therapist said to me.

1

I was about to turn thirty, living in Chicago, recording recurring nightmares I now understand as a symptom of post-traumatic stress.

Confusing. Shame-filled.
Dysregulating.

And deeply women-centered in their themes.

2

I came into her office tearful, a total mess, and handed her my dreams.

She offered me an Akashic records session instead.

3

I left feeling unseen. But with a whisper in the back of my mind —

or maybe the back of my womb —

Dreams are not nonsense.

4

This is important work.

And women deserve more than that.

That moment is why I do this.

5
Here’s the surprising part

This work is not about interpreting your dreams.

I don’t think anyone actually wants their dreams interpreted by a stranger.

6

What helps is learning to find meaning in your own dreams —

and learning to listen, in your body, when someone shares theirs.

A dream is unedited material from your subconscious. It’s a language only you speak.

7
🌙

The work is staying fluent in it across a lifetime.
